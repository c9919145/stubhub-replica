Frequently asked questions

Browsing and buying on TicketVault is simple. Search for the event you want to attend, choose the tickets that work best for you based on section, quantity, and price, then complete your order. You'll receive your tickets via email or in the TicketVault app before the event.

The TicketVault Buyer Protection ensures:

  • Your tickets will be delivered in time for the event
  • Your tickets will be valid for entry
  • If an event is cancelled with no rescheduled date, you'll receive a 120% credit or full cash refund
  • You'll be taken care of from purchase to entry with our customer service

Selling on TicketVault is easy. List your tickets by entering the event details, setting your price, and choosing your delivery method. Once your tickets sell, you'll receive payment. TicketVault handles the transaction safely and securely.

Delivery timing depends on the event and the seller. Most tickets are delivered electronically and are available in your TicketVault account or app well before the event. You'll receive a notification when your tickets are ready.

Yes. If an event is cancelled with no rescheduled date, you'll receive a 120% credit of your purchase price or a full cash refund, whichever you prefer. If an event is rescheduled, your tickets will still be valid for the new date.

TicketVault guarantees that tickets sold as a group will be together. When you purchase multiple tickets in a single transaction, they will be in the same section and as close to each other as possible.
